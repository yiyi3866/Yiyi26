* General Information

Figures of Aureole Velocity, Deformation and Temperature.

Figure_1: Snapshots of Inverse Distance Weighting (IDW) interpolation velocity profile with
the emplacement duration of 100 kyr.

Figure_2_3_4_5: Figure 2-5, theoretical curves of how different parameters affect peak temperature profiles in country rocks.
   - Figure_2: Regional background temperature.
   - Figure_3: Emplacement duration.
   - Figure_4: Aureole width.
   - Figure_5: Velocity settings.

Figure_7: Amount of shortening deduced from field measurements and the model fit under the Inverse Distance Weighting (IDW) interpolation velocity

Figure_9: Observed peak metamorphic temperatures of 42 country-rock samples compared with the modeled thermal profile. 


* This code works in Rstudio only.
